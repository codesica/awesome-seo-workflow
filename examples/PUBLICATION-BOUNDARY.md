# Evidence and publication boundary

## What these examples establish

The included fixtures are synthetic and can illustrate a diagnostic method. They establish no production deployment, indexing status, traffic change, ranking improvement, or causal effect. A passing local check must be labeled “fixture check passed,” never “indexed by Google.”

A public HTTP observation can establish the status, headers and body observed at a particular time. It cannot establish ownership, private Search Console status, historical indexing, or permission to republish account records. Record the exact URL, time, method and limits whenever adding such an observation.

## Real evidence requiring a separate publication decision

Before including a real project, obtain approval for the exact selected excerpt, named or anonymized attribution, destination public repository/site, and any consequential performance claim. Review:

- Search Console screenshots, property identifiers, inspection results and exports.
- Analytics, search queries, traffic, impressions, revenue and date ranges.
- Private issue threads, commits, audit notes, emails, account names and internal paths.
- Customer or third-party content, licensed assets and identifying combinations of details.

Redaction does not itself authorize publication. Remove tokens, emails, personal data, internal identifiers and unrelated browser UI; review image metadata and the final rendered output. Publish only an approved standalone excerpt with provenance, limitations and a check date. Never mirror a private repository, its history, or a folder wholesale into the public repository.

## Acceptance before a real case is published

1. Exact artifact and audience approved.
2. Final sanitized artifact reviewed; secrets and identifying leftovers checked.
3. Claims match the evidence and distinguish observation from inference.
4. No guaranteed ranking or indexing claims.
5. Keep raw evidence and approval records outside this public example directory.

Until these conditions hold, use these synthetic cases instead.
