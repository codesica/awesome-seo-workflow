# Discovery and promotion

Bring a useful page to the right people and distinguish traffic sources.

## Entry requirement

Enter with a functioning public page; do not broaden private-content access as a promotion tactic.

## Goal

Complete a bounded discovery plan: reachable internally, appropriately shared externally, with search and referral traffic distinguished.

## Concepts first

- SEO improves search understanding and discovery. Community, social, email, and ads are promotion. They can complement SEO, but a social visit is not an organic search click.

- A backlink is another site linking to yours. Genuine relevant references can aid discovery; ranking-manipulation links and spam are risky.

## Inputs

- A checked public URL, intended audience, available channels, and their rules.

- Explicit scope: drafts only or approval to send to specified destinations. Preparing copy does not authorize mass outreach.

## Actions

### 05.1 Add an internal discovery route

Add a useful link from a relevant section or existing article with descriptive text. Walk the path to the page; do not rely solely on a sitemap or site search. Use your normal publishing process for changes.

Output: Entry page, anchor text, target URL, and verification.

### 05.2 Pick one relevant external context

Choose one community, subscribed audience, or collaborator already interested in the problem. Read sharing rules. Explain what the page answers and disclose your relationship. If no suitable context exists, retain a draft rather than chasing a link count.

Output: One channel rationale and a contextual sharing draft.

### 05.3 Review before sharing

Confirm destination, wording, platform rules, and permission. Send only within authorization. Avoid guaranteed-ranking link packages, unnatural exchanges, and unsolicited automated blasts. Separate sent items from drafts.

Output: Approved distribution record or clearly unsent draft.

### 05.4 Separate channel outcomes

Use available analytics or channel evidence for visits and feedback. If using UTM tags, consistently tag external campaigns, not internal navigation, and never include personal data. Without analytics access, state not measurable rather than inventing numbers.

Output: Channel log: date, destination, draft/sent, tags, observable feedback, and unknowns.

## Tools

- Site editor, browser, and authorized distribution channels. Existing analytics are optional; new tracking must respect privacy and consent requirements.

## Deliverables

- Discovery log with one verified internal entry and one external plan, accurately labeled by execution status. Sending is not mandatory.

## Acceptance criteria

- The page is reachable from relevant internal content, external plans fit the audience and rules, and organic search, referral/social visits, and business outcomes are recorded separately.

## Stop conditions

- Do not send when promotion is prohibited, authorization is absent, or the page fails its promise. Investigate and correct misleading claims before expanding distribution.

## Next step

Bring release dates, channel records, and feedback to stage 6.
