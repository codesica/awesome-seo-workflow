# Learn the full SEO workflow, starting with one real question

A beginner path through need, page planning, content, publication, discovery, and iteration. Understand the task, do the work, and leave a checkable output.

## Start where you are

- [I do not know what to create](/en/stages/01-search-opportunity/)：Start with real questions and search intent.

- [I have a topic, but no page plan](/en/stages/02-page-plan/)：Turn the reader task into a page brief.

- [I have a brief and need content](/en/stages/03-content-production/)：Turn evidence into usable content.

- [My page is live or ready to publish](/en/stages/04-publish-check/)：Check the actual page without repeating earlier stages.

- [My page works, but people do not know it](/en/stages/05-discovery-promotion/)：Add discovery paths and choose relevant promotion.

- [I have pages and data; what should change?](/en/stages/06-measure-iterate/)：Diagnose from evidence and return to the relevant stage.



SEO helps search engines understand content and readers find pages through search. Social, community, and email distribution are promotion; this path connects them without treating them as organic search ranking.



Learn to choose a question, brief a page, create reliable content, check publication, plan distribution, and interpret data. No indexing, ranking, traffic, or revenue is promised.



No website yet? Complete stages 1–3 in documents and use public teaching examples for stage 4. Accounts, domains, and deployment are separate tasks.



Tutorials explain why and how. Agent Skills assist with bounded repeatable work. Each skill should declare inputs, outputs, permissions, risks, and test evidence. Installation does not complete SEO or grant publication or account access.



This six-stage curriculum is checked against official sources, but has not been executed end-to-end on your site. The existing crawlability, sitemap, and page-check tutorials cover stage 4 only; new stages are not proven growth results.



Take one familiar question through a loop. Example: how a beginner can start basil seeds indoors. This demonstrates the method only; demand and search opportunity are not validated.



## The six-stage path



### 1. Need and search opportunity

Identify a real need before choosing a topic.

Opportunity card fields: audience, context, primary question, intent, sources, dates, region/language, distinctive help, volume with provenance or unknown, and next step.



### 2. Page plan

Give one page a clear job.

Page brief: audience, outcome, primary question, format, target URL, create/update rationale, title, outline, evidence, inbound link, next step, and acceptance criteria.



### 3. Content production

Help readers finish a task, not just find keywords.

Content package: body, title, description, assets/rights, links, fact log, locale versions, and reader-test notes.



### 4. Publish and technical checks

Separate being live, crawlable, indexed, and ranked.

Release record: version, final URL, time, rollback point, checks, evidence, unverified account-only items, and recheck trigger.



### 5. Discovery and promotion

Bring a useful page to the right people and distinguish traffic sources.

Discovery log with one verified internal entry and one external plan, accurately labeled by execution status. Sending is not mandatory.



### 6. Measurement and iteration

Locate the problem, then change one worthwhile thing.

A baseline, improvement card, and review with source, time range, filters, and unknowns for every metric.
