# Content production

Help readers finish a task, not just find keywords.

## Entry requirement

Enter with a brief; existing content can enter with a specific improvement goal.

## Goal

Produce a verifiable, useful content package ready for a publisher.

## Concepts first

- Original value can be tested steps, clear examples, a comparison method, or a tool; it need not be a new discovery. Citation is not permission to rewrite someone’s whole article.

- The title must match the page’s promise. A meta description summarizes the page; Google may generate a different snippet from the content.

## Inputs

- A page brief, reliable sources, licensed assets, and any necessary qualified reviewer.

## Actions

### 03.1 Draft around the task

State who the page is for, what they will achieve, and what they need. For each step include input, action, and expected result. Add troubleshooting to fragile steps. Use verifiable examples, never invented customer outcomes.

Output: A complete draft with demonstrations explicitly labeled as examples.

### 03.2 Check facts and sources

Check numbers, product steps, dates, and causal claims against primary sources. Test actions you claim to have tested and record the environment and result; otherwise say untested. AI may help organize, but generated references are not evidence.

Output: Fact log: claim, source, review date, and test status.

### 03.3 Prepare the page package

Prepare an accurate page title, short description, readable headings, and descriptive links; explain necessary terms. Place images near relevant text and supply useful text alternatives. Identify authors/reviewers and substantive update dates without faking freshness.

Output: Publishable body, metadata, assets, and annotations.

### 03.4 Run a beginner walkthrough

Ask a target reader or someone unfamiliar with the project to follow the page and record where they get stuck. Review terminology, links, and examples separately in each language rather than translating labels only. Fix major gaps before handoff; do not pad to a word-count target.

Output: Walkthrough findings, fixes, and unresolved questions.

## Tools

- Text editor/CMS draft and browser. AI is an optional assistant, not a fact source.

## Deliverables

- Content package: body, title, description, assets/rights, links, fact log, locale versions, and reader-test notes.

## Acceptance criteria

- The promised task is achievable, important facts are traceable, no metrics or reviews are fabricated, assets are usable, and both languages contain substantive content.

## Stop conditions

- Pause publication if core steps fail, sources are insufficient, or rights are unclear. SEO presentation cannot remedy unreviewed high-risk medical, legal, or financial advice.

## Next step

Once accepted, proceed to stage 4. A finished draft is not a live page.
